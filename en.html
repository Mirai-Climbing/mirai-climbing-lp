What makes Mirai Climbing different

1

Neutral—no single style favored

Mirai Climbing is designed to stay neutral: bouldering or lead, gym or outdoor, casual or serious.
We don’t assume one style or level. We want you to use it in the way that fits you now.
The app aims to be a flat, neutral space that works for every climber’s distance and goals.

2

Close to the developer

Mirai Climbing is an indie app. That’s why we care a lot about what users say.
Your feedback shapes what we learn from real use and what we improve next.
“I’d like this feature” or “this is hard to use”—that kind of input is exactly what drives the next update.

3

Built to grow with you

Mirai Climbing is still in beta. Features and UI will keep changing.
We want it to be an app that grows with climbers, not a finished product.
We hope you enjoy being part of that process.

Mirai Climbing isn’t for one type of climber.
It’s for you—whoever is climbing right now.
